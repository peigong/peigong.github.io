#  The forbidden city

Ladies and gentlemen, my friends, I will be your guide today.

And it's my great honor to show you around the forbidden city.

We are now in front of the meridian gate, And i'd like to give you a brief introduction of the forbidden city.

## 0

The forbidden city was located in the center of beijing. It was built in 1420 during the reign of the emperor yongle of the ming dynasty. The emperor yongle made up his mind to move the capital from nanjing to beijing.

In 1987, the forbidden city was listed as the world culture heritage.

The forbidden city is the largest man-made palace in the world today. It is 961 meters long from south to North, and 753 meters wide from east to west. The forbidden city is  Surrounded by 10 meters high city walls,  And 52 meters wide river running around it.

The forbidden city was called the purple forbidden city. Why is That?

The Chinese emperor believed that he was the son of the god, and the place where he lived should be decorated  In purple as well.

Secondly, no commoners were allowed to enter it. That's why it was forbidden. Now, you will know  why we call it forbidden city, right?

By the way, the forbidden city is also called the palace museum, because we converted it into a museum in 1925.

The forbidden city is located on the central axis of beijing. In 1925 the forbidden city was opened to the public.

## 1

First of all, let's take a look at the meridian gate.

The meridian gate, more commonly known as the five phoenix tower because of the five pavilions on Top,  Is the southern entrance to the Imperial palace.

After that, we will see a river in front of us, That is the inner golden water river. Over the river, There are five bridges.

Keep going and we can see a pair of  Bronze lions. The lion on the east holding  A ball is the male lion, While west one playing with Cub is the female one.

And then you will see the gate of supreme harmony. In the ming dynasty. This was the place for the  Emperors to  Hold imperial audiences.

Entering the gate of supreme harmony, you will see an open space with an area of 30000 square meters.

And if you look straight ahead, you will see the triple marble terrace in distance, On which stands the hall of supreme harmony.

Forbidden city is mainly divided into two major parts, The outer-court and the inner-court.

The outer court are composed of three  Main big halls on the middle axis,The hall of supreme harmony, the hall of middle harmony, the hall of Preserving harmony.

## 3

I'd like to Give a brief introduction of the hall of supreme harmony.

The hall of supreme harmony is the largest、Tallest and most important structure with the highest level in the forbidden city.

It was first built in 1420 Called the hall of Heavenly worship, And was renamed as the hall of imperial supremacy in the ming jiajing's period,  And the present one was rebuilt in 1695 Named as the hall of supreme harmony.

The hall of supreme harmony is 11 rooms in Width and 5 rooms in depth.

Once we walk along the staircase of the marble terrace,  You will see 18 incense  Burners,  And they symbolized 18  Provinces of the qing dynasty.

Now please follow me to take a look inside the hall of supreme harmony. You will see the gilded throne  Carved with 13 dragons, Placed on the platform in the middle of hall.
