# The Tian'anmen Square

We are now at the center of the Square, and I'd like to give you a brief introduction about it

Tian'anmen and Tian'anmen Square are located in the center of Beijing. Tian'anmen was first built in 1420 during the Ming Dynasty. At that time, it was served as the main entrance to the Imperial City, and the Tian'anmen Square was a 'T' shaped square.

Today, Tian'anmen and Tian'anmen Square have been completely turned into a new look. Especially since Chairman Mao proclaimed to the whole world the founding of the People's Republic of China on October 1st 1949, Tian'anmen has been the symbol of new China.

Now, the Square is the largest city center square in the world, and it attracts millions of people from all over the world every year.

Tian'anmen means 'the Gate of Heavenly Peace' in English and it is located in the center of Beijing and stands on the northern end of Tian'anmen Square.

During the Ming and Qing dynasties, the gate was served as the main entrance to the former Imperial City.

Today, the huge  portrait of Chairman Mao is hanging in the middle of tian'anmen Rostrum. The portrait is 6 meters high, 4.6 meters wide, and weighs about 1.5 tons. In the middle, between the double eaves is the National Emblem.

There are two slogans on each side, one is 'Long live to the People's Republic of China', the other one is 'Long live to the Great Unity of the Peoples of the World'.

On the west side of Tian'anmen stands Zhongshan Park, formerly called the Altar of Land and Grain. On the east side is the Working People's Cultural Palace that used to be the Supreme Ancestral Temple.

Besides those, we also have the Outer Golden Water Bridges, which now is a part of the Central Axis.

I will give you more Information later.

Ladies and gentlemen, Tian'anmen Square is situated south of Tian'anmen Rostrum, 880 meters long from north to south, and 500 meters wide from east to west, with a total area of 44 hectares. Tian'anmen Square is the largest city center square in the world.

Let's take a look at the Tian'anmen Square, on October 1st, 1949, Chairman Mao standing on Tian'anmen Rostrum, solemnly declared to the whole world, 'The People's Republic of China has been  Founded'. The Chinese people have ever since stood up.

Tian'anmen Square was enlarged in the 1950s, and some important buildings were also built on the square, such as: the Great Hall of People to the west, the National Museum(Museum of Chinese History and the Museum of the Chinese Revolution) to the east, and the Monument to the People's Heroes is standing in the center with Chairman Mao's Mausoleum right behind it.

Please take a look at our National Flag. Our National Flag is red in color with five yellow stars. The big yellow star in the center represents the Communist Party of China and the four smaller ones symbolize the masses of the Chinese people surrounding closely to the Communist Party of China.

The first National Flag, 5 meters long and 3 meters wide, raised by Chairman Mao on October 1st 1949 is now housed in the National Museum.

Okay, ladies and gentlemen, now let's take a look around this area.

In the center of the square stands the Monument to the People's Heroes. The construction began in 1952 and was completed in 1958.

On the front side of the monument, there is an engraved inscription in Chinese written by Chairman Mao, 'Eternal Glory to the People's Heroes'. On the back of the monument, is an article also written by Chairman Mao, but the calligraphy was written by our late Premier Zhou.

The bottom layer of the monument is inlaid with eight big pieces of white marble, relieves covered out of white marble, showing the Chinese historical events since 1840.

There are, For example: the Burning of Opium in 1840, the Jingtian Uprising in Guangxi of 1851, the Wuchang Uprising in Hubei of 1911,  the May 4th Movement of 1919, the May 30th Movement of 1925, the Nanchang Uprising in Jiangxi of 1927.

After that, please take a look at the building to its south, that is Chairman Mao's Mausoleum.

Chairman Mao's Mausoleum is located to the south of the Monument to the People's Heroes.

Chairman Mao, the founder of the People's Republic of China and chairman of the Communist Party of China.

The whole construction took 6 months and was officially open to the public on September 9th, 1977, just one year after his death.

There are three main halls inside the mausoleum. They are the North Hall, the Central Hall and the South Hall.

To the west side of the Square, lies the Great Hall of People. As one of the top 10 constructions, the Great Hall of People was completed in 1959 within 10 months.

Across the Great Hall of People lies the National Museum, and it was also one of the Top 10 major buildings in 1959. The National Museum was founded in 2003, merging from the National Museum of Chinese History and the National Museum of Chinese Revolution.

To the south of Chairman Mao's Mausoleum sits the Zhengyang Gate. Zhengyang Gate, commonly known as Qianmen, is located on the Central Axis of Beijing, at the southern end of Tian'anmen Square and the northern end of Qianmen Street. It was the main gate of the inner city of Beijing during the Ming and Qing dynasties.
