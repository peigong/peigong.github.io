# The Great Wall

Ladies and gentlemen, my friends. I will be your guide today.

And it is my great honor to show you around the Great Wall.

We are now 30 minutes to the Badaling section of the Great Wall, and now I'd like to give you some information about the history of it.

The Great Wall symbolizing China ancient civilization, is one of the most famous grand and splendid ancient construction wonders in the world.

In 1987, it was listed as the World  Cultural Heritage.

Construction of the Great Wall first began in the 7th  century BC. At that time, it was called the Spring and Autumn Period. The first section of wall, which appeared in China was built by the Kingdom Qi and the Kingdom Chu. They had a high wall which was called 'square wall' or 'square city', so it was also known as the Qi Wall and the Chu Wall.

In Spring and Autumn Period and Warring State Period, seven states become the most powerful states( namely: Qi, Chu, Yan, Han, Zhao, Wei and Qin States), they are called 'Seven Powers'. In order to defend themselves, all kingdoms followed the example of State Qi and State Chu, and had sections of high walls constructed around their on territories for self protection.

And after that, Large-scale construction of the Great Wall started and the construction of that was  concentrated in the Qin, Han and Ming dynasties.

In 221 BC, Qin shihuangdi defeated the other six kingdoms and unified China. Since the six states were all conquered by him, the first united feudal empire in Chinese history came into being. He gave himself the title: the first Emperor of the Qin Dynasty, Emperor Qin Shihuang.

He linked up the separate sections of high walls, built by different kingdoms, into the Great Wall.

The linking work took 10 years to finish until the end of the Qin Dynasty. When it was finished, the total length of the Great Wall was 7000 kilometers long. The Qin Great Wall started from Lintao, Gansu Province in the west and in Liaodong, Liaoning Province in the east.

After Qin is Han.

Large-scale construction of the Great Wall was ongoing throughout the reign of Emperor Wudi in the Han Dynasty.

The Han Great Wall started from the Liaodong Peninsula in the east, and ended at the foot of the Tianshan Mountain in Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region, with a total length of 10000 kilometers.

The last large-scale project on the Great Wall was carried out in the Ming Dynasty. After Zhu Yuanzhang established the Ming Dynasty, he sent his chief general Xuda up to the north immediately and started the reconstruction of the Great Wall on the basis of the Qin Great wall.

The whole project of the Ming Great Wall took more than 200 years to complete, and there were 18 large-scale constructions of the Great Wall in the Ming Dynasty. After that, the total length of the Ming Great Wall was 8851.8 kilometers from Yalu River in Liaoning Province in the east, to Jiayuguan Pass in Gansu Province in the west.

During the Ming Dynasty two important passes were reconstructed on the Ming Great Wall. One is Shanhaiguan Pass, which is located in Qinhuangdao, in the eastern part of the Ming Great Wall. The other one is Jiayuguan Pass, the western part of the Ming Great Wall in Gansu Province.

Ladies and gentlemen, there are many sections of the Great Wall in Beijing, today we will focus on the Juyong Pass and Badaling section.

Now we are entering Nankou, and from Nankou to Badaling, there are altogether 4 passes, which are, Nankou Pass, Juyongguan Pass, Shangguan Pass and Badaling Pass. All the 4 passes are located in this valley, which is about 20 kilometers long, so this valley is called '40-li Pass Valley', and the Pass Valley in Chinese is called Guangou.

Okay, ladies and gentlemen, please first take a look at this huge wooden plaque, which is hung on the southern side of the Pass, with three characters meaning 'Juyongguan'. According to historical records, Emperor Qinshihuang forced many conscripts to build this part of the Great Wall.

Okay, ladies and gentlemen, please take a look at this construction.

This is an ancient marble platform known as Cloud Terrace. It was built during the Yuan Dynasty inside Juyong Pass.

Now let's have a look at the carvings.

In the middle edge of the archway, there is a carving of a 'golden winged bird'.

Inside the arched passage of Cloud Terrace, there are carved in relief buddhist images on the wall.

There are also Buddhist scriptures inscribed on the stonewall in 6 languages. They are extremely valuable to the study of Buddhism and ancient languages.

Now we are 10 minutes away from the Badaling.

The Badaling Great Wall is about 75 kilometers northwest of Beijing, and it is the best preserved part of the Great Wall.

Badaling Pass was an outpost of Juyongguan Pass. It used to be more important than Juyongguan Pass in the defense of Beijing. An old saying can be the best description, 'If one man guards the pass, 10000 can't get through'.
